Proof, not promises

Our scanner is open source.

Run it on your own code before you ever talk to us. If it finds nothing, you don't need us — and you'll know either way in under a minute.

audit.py 
from pathlib import Path
from scanner import scan, print_report

findings = scan(Path("./your-api"))

critical = [f for f in findings if f.severity == "CRITICAL"]
for f in critical:
    print(f"{f.file}:{f.line}  {f.algorithm}")

print_report(findings, root=Path("./your-api"))
# → 14 critical · 6 warnings · 2 quantum-safe

Behind the scanner

A human reads every finding.

Automated scanners flag; they don't judge. Every hit in your report is triaged by a person who asks the questions a regex can't: is this key live, what data does it protect, and how long does that data need to stay secret? You get findings that matter, not noise.
